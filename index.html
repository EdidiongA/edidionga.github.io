About

I am a machine learning and infrastructure engineer based in Lagos, Nigeria. For fifteen years I have been the sole technical owner of the enterprise infrastructure at the West African Institute for Financial and Economic Management (WAIFEM), a regional institution that trains central bankers and finance officials across five countries in partnership with the IMF, the World Bank and the African Development Bank. I run everything from identity management and virtualization to fibre-optic and satellite links, with round-the-clock accountability.

In 2024 I completed an MSc in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, specializing in Interactive Intelligence: machine learning, knowledge-based AI and human-computer interaction. Since then I have turned that training into applied work: a Kaggle silver medal in geosteering prediction, an accepted CLEF working note in bioacoustics, an open-source cost-aware evaluation library, and a set of production AI systems that ship with their evaluation harnesses and failure taxonomies.

What connects it all is a conviction that AI systems should be honest about what they do not know. I build for abstention, instrumentation and evaluation first, and for the real conditions of the places I have worked: constrained bandwidth, tight budgets and users who need to check an answer, not trust it.

I am open to senior roles in MLOps, ML platform engineering, applied machine learning and digital transformation, whether remote or with relocation.

Publications

Peer-reviewed and workshop papers, with preprints where available.

2026
Accepted, CLEF 2026 Working Notes

Leveraging Public Resources as a Late Entrant in BirdCLEF+ 2026: An Empirical Study of Embedding-Space Ensemble Diversity and Its Limits

Anwanane, E. CLEF 2026 Working Notes (LifeCLEF / BirdCLEF+), CEUR-WS Proceedings.

Documents a controlled negative result: a custom head on Perch v2 embeddings looked like a strong ensemble member on every standalone metric yet reduced the blend at every weight. When downstream models share one frozen representation, their outputs can decorrelate freely while label-relevant information stays bottlenecked upstream. Link added on publication.

Blind-Zone TVT Prediction in Horizontal Wells: A Structural-Coordinate Tracker with Deployment-Side Dose Calibration, and a Three-Way Study of Why Leaderboards Disagree

Anwanane, E. Preprint from the Kaggle ROGII Wellbore Geology Prediction competition (silver medal, 241 of 6,125).
